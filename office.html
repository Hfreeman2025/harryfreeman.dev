How this page works

The cabinet is HTML and CSS in three dimensions: every drawer is a button and every folder a link, so the directory, the keyboard and a screen reader reach the same ten tools. The loops in the folders were recorded from the real tools in a silent headless browser and are labelled recorded; Open launches the tool live. The floor plan is drawn from the same plan as the studio model, and the dots replay the build timeline.

Reception runs ask-the-record entirely in your browser. Keyword search and the suggested questions need no download; your own question loads the meaning model on first use, about 50 MB, served from this site and cached. Your question never leaves the page.
