Third-party code, data and type on Harry Freeman's site

The site's own code is under the MIT licence. It uses, unmodified:

DATA

  Carbon intensity of Great Britain's electricity grid
    NESO Carbon Intensity API, https://carbonintensity.org.uk/
    © National Energy System Operator (NESO). Licensed under Creative Commons
    Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
    Used by the grid sign and the room's light (the current national
    half-hour, /intensity, one request when the page opens and again after
    each half-hour), and by the radio while it plays (/intensity and
    /generation). Shown as published; nothing is cached as current.

AUDIO

  The voice at the door ("Hello, welcome! ... Come and explore."),
  public/audio/door-voice.webm (Opus) and door-voice.m4a (AAC), 3.23 s.
    Generated offline with Kokoro-82M (onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX at
    1939ad2a8e416c0acfeecc08a694d14ef25f2231, q8, voice bm_fable, speed 1.05),
    Apache-2.0, through kokoro-js 1.2.1 (Apache-2.0) on @huggingface/transformers
    3.8.1 (Apache-2.0). Its phonemiser bundles espeak-ng (GPL-3.0); it was run
    offline only, and only the generated audio is on this site, so no GPL code
    is. Each sentence was generated separately, silence-trimmed, joined with a
    0.35 s gap and loudness-normalised to -18 LUFS with ffmpeg. The master is
    audio/door-voice.wav in the source. SHA-256 prefixes: m4a deb38608759ca7ac,
    webm 6a11056ea07e5fe5.

  The knock is synthesised in the page (WebAudio): no recording.
